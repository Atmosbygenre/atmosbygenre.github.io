The playlists

Pick a genre, press shuffle

Each one opens in TIDAL, where you can follow it. They're big, so shuffle works better than playing from the top. The bar shows each playlist's size against the largest.

    Only real Atmos mixes

    A track goes in only when TIDAL itself tags it as Dolby Atmos. Each song appears once, so you won't hear both the deluxe and standard copy, or the explicit and clean version.

    Updated every week

    A weekly update checks TIDAL's new Atmos releases and re-checks each artist's catalog about once a month, adding anything new.

    What you need

    A TIDAL subscription and a device TIDAL plays Dolby Atmos on. TIDAL's help center lists the supported apps and devices. Atmos tracks show a Dolby Atmos badge in the app.
